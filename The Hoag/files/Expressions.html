Expressions

← Wiki Home

The common speech of the colonized galaxies has evolved a rich layer of slang, compressed affirmations, and social shorthand. Most expressions follow a doubling convention — repeating a word to intensify or disambiguate its meaning. MultiSpeakUniversal is the default language of colonized space, but these expressions are cross-cultural and widely understood.

Affirmations & Agreements

FineFine

Where saying Fine alone could be ambiguous, FineFine signals that something is genuinely, unambiguously fine. A statement of total homeostasis and mutual understanding — a clear signal to move on.

GoGo

Go ahead — no challenge. Let's get moving.
An enthusiastic affirmation of permission or momentum. No obstacle, no hesitation.

KoolKool

Like totally Kool, Dude — like totally.
An emphatic expression of approval and enthusiasm. Carries a retro-slang flavour echoing OldeNorthAmerican idiom.

PositivePositive

A confirmation that what is known is verified as truth. Stronger than a simple yes — it closes down ambiguity entirely.

TrueTrue

Something is not merely true, but unequivocally true. No room for doubt or interpretation.

TrueFact

A spoken assertion backed by proven theory. Distinguished from opinion — TrueFact invokes measurable reality.

ThankThank

Genuine, emphatic gratitude. An expression of deep appreciation for another person's sharing, character, or knowledge.

Negatives & Tension

ColdCold

Really cold air. A cold body. Freezing temperatures. By extension: a mindset that is emotionally frozen or shut down.

GastZen

False peacefulness. A situation or action that causes anxiety or fear beneath a calm exterior. Also: surprise — the sudden rupture of assumed calm.

SquareMattersNot

The negative emotions associated with misunderstanding or disrespect are unwarranted here. All outside the square is relevant. A declaration of openness and dismissal of petty conflict.

Action & Energy

Zizz · to Zazz

High slang. A commoner vernacular term for doing something fun, spontaneous, and energetic. Used as a sentence-ender that signals action — a command to a fellow. To do with energy.

Example use: "Let's just Zazz and sort it out after."

Concepts Sometimes Used as Expressions

FactKnow

To assume a conversational stance that a subject is fully mastered — projecting expertise, whether earned or not.

BlackFreeSpace

(Also an object/system — see Objects & Technology)
Used conversationally: to Kiv emotionally — to share something deeply personal in a private, encrypted space.

Pod

(Also a technology — see Objects & Technology)
Used as a social expression: to be Pod with someone — to share the same emotion simultaneously. An act of total empathic alignment.
