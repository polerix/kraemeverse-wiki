# Characters · Humans (Genetic Originals)

[← Wiki Home](Home)

Genetic Originals (GOs) are humans whose reproduction and biology remain unchanged from 21st-century practices. They carry no implants, genetic modifications, or cortical enhancements — though some may later choose augmentation. Many GOs belong to **TroughCults** or other communities that actively reject technology.

---

## Soo Lingg
**Full name:** Soo Lo Tang Neet Mahatsamautman Lingg  
**Nicknames:** "Soo Bee," "BujuButt"  
**Stock:** HomeWorld GO  
**Affiliation:** Lhasa Monastery · YungwalaCultBubble

A human monk acolyte without implants or enhancements of any kind. Soo is a student of **Master Hun** and a devoted follower of old Tibetan traditions. An orphan of a culture that rejected all technology, Soo was raised entirely within the monastery system.

Soo appears to exhibit extraordinary empathetic and telepathic powers — abilities consistent with the monastery's teaching of **SixthSensing**. Whether these abilities are supernatural or a highly refined form of human perception remains open.

> *See also:* [SixthSense](Concepts#sixthsense), [PrefrontaryCorticalPneumonicLoop](Concepts#prefrontarycorticalpneumonicloop), [TroughCults](Concepts#troughcults)

## Master Hun
**Role:** Head Monk, Lhasa Monastery  
**Affiliation:** YungwalaCultBubble · Himalayan TroughCult

The senior monk at the Lhasa Monastery and Soo Lingg's personal master. Master Hun is responsible for training Soo in **SixthSensing** — the meditative discipline of empathic and telepathic perception. He represents the highest authority within the monastic tradition.

> *See also:* [SixthSense](Concepts#sixthsense), [MyScriptureBeing](Concepts#myscripturebeing)

## Ffookhago
**Nickname:** "SkankHole"  
**Stock:** GO Nipponese  
**Status:** *Later augmented — see [Characters · Augmented](Characters-Augmented)*

Soo Lingg's childhood friend, raised together at the Lhasa Monastery. Though Ffookhago began life as a pure Genetic Original, he departed the monastery and subsequently underwent a series of medical and technological procedures that enhanced his brain's capacity for reasoning and knowledge storage. His transition makes him a bridge figure between the GO and augmented worlds.

## Qerid M'khogij
**Stock:** Bali Original (GO)  
**Status:** *Cortically enhanced — see [Characters · Augmented](Characters-Augmented)*  
**Occupation:** Paleontological researcher; HighEd graduate

A Bali-born Genetic Original who has since accepted cortical enhancement. Qerid seeks ancient life fossils on planets within the HomeWorld solar system. As a **HighEd** graduate she is fluent in the technologies of the era and uses them extensively in her research.

---

## Hunfe' Gvuyo
**Stock:** Bali Original (GO)  
**Relation:** Qerid M'khogij's childhood friend

Qerid's closest friend from childhood. Hunfe' Gvuyo is the character who initiates the **WorldDate** event that draws the core group of characters together.

---

## Mooma Geao
**Relation:** Qerid's aunt  
**Occupation:** Co-owner, *Dementias* nightclub (Bali)

Half-owner of the Bali nightclub *Dementias*. Mooma is deeply proud of her family's genetically unbroken lineage — 200 consecutive generations of Genetic Originals. This pride defines her identity and her investment in the club's philosophy.

---

## Uncle Nitrand
**Relation:** Qerid's uncle  
**Occupation:** Co-owner, *Dementias* nightclub (Bali)

The other half-owner of *Dementias*. Uncle Nitrand refuses to permit any technology inside the club — a deliberate design choice that includes integrating live wildlife as part of the venue's atmosphere. His philosophy echoes the GO commitment to unmediated experience.
