# Characters · Personates

[← Wiki Home](Home)

**Personates** are the Genetically Enhanced (GEs) — progeny of Old Earth genetic manipulation programs that produced enhanced offshoots of the original Homo Sapiens. They are advanced humans, typically bred or modified for survival on specific colony worlds, and are found throughout the colonized galaxies. Personates exist on a continuum from mildly modified to radically adapted.

## Jixa
**Origin:** Colony planet Masaru  
**Height:** Three meters  
**Occupation:** Enceladus Atmospheric Regeneration Project  
**Relation:** Qerid M'khogij's long-term friend

A towering Personate from the colony world Masaru, Jixa's three-meter stature reflects the physical adaptations common among those engineered for non-HomeWorld environments. Jixa works on the **Enceladus Atmospheric Regeneration Project** — a large-scale terraforming or atmospheric restoration effort associated with Enceladus, one of the moons of Saturn in the Sol system.

Jixa maintains a long-standing friendship with **Qerid M'khogij**, connecting the colony-world Personate community with the HomeWorld GO social circle.

## Teragram KcoCed
**Classification:** Anonymous Orbii · Spacer Personate  
**Status:** Mystery person

Little is known about Teragram KcoCed. They are identified as an **Orbii** — a Personate adapted to zero-gravity and vacuum conditions in the Sol system — but operate under a veil of anonymity. Their role in events remains deliberately obscure.
