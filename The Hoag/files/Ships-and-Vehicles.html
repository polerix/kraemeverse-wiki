Ships & Vehicles

← Wiki Home

Transportation in the HOAG universe operates across three scales: personal, public, and interstellar. Antigravity is the standard propulsion method for atmospheric craft. Interstellar and intergalactic travel relies on SMOSA anomalies and TransferStation infrastructure.

Personal Vehicles

Flitter

A personal, single-occupant flying vehicle. The private car of the era — compact, atmospheric, individually operated. Antigravity-propelled.

Public Transit

GuildedGlobusFlyers

Public ovoid flying craft that use antigravity propulsion. The mass-transit system of HomeWorld and colony settlements — equivalent to buses or rail, but aerial. Recognizable by their globular shape.

Infrastructure-Scale Transport

InfinitySpans

Floating bridges that often cross entire oceans, suspended and maintained by antigravity systems. While not vehicles themselves, InfinitySpans are the fixed-route corridors along which atmospheric traffic flows at continental scale.

See also: Objects & Technology · InfinitySpans

Interstellar & Intergalactic Travel

No named vessels appear in the current lexicon. Interstellar travel is facilitated by SMOSA (Super Massive Orbiting Singularity Anomaly) distortions and TransferStation infrastructure that generates SpaceTyme corridors.

  • Galaxy-scale hops became possible in the 26th century
  • Reliable accuracy was achieved by the 32nd century
  • The GalacticPig Super-Group represents the full scope of colonized space reached through this system

See also: SMOSA, SpaceTyme, GalacticPig Super-Group
