HOAG INDEX GLOSSARY         APRIL DRAFT          LEGACE/ KRAEMER
HOAG CHARACTERS
( By order of appearance )




Soo Lingg - (Soo Lo Tang Neet Mahatsamautman Lingg) “Soo Bee'' aka “BujuButt.” A  Human genetic original (GO) monk acolyte, without implants or enhancements. Soo is a follower of old Tibetan traditions,a  student of Master Hun,  an orphan of a culture which rejected all technology. Soo appears to exhibit supernatural empathetic and telepathic powers.


Server – An Alien Pure Electric entity which connects every Person and Personate on HomeWorld individually, or on mass. Server also operates all systems on HomeWorld and in the HomeWorld Solar system.The Server entity appeared in the Milky Way galaxy at about the time humanity discovered Hoag.


Ambassador Truk – The Ambassador is a Hoag Superspecies alien from the 92 degree in the Hoag galaxy. The Ambassadors home plant is named Crawl. The Ambassador is a Motive Hoag and is likely several million years old. The Ambassador was brought to HomeWorld as Hoag ambassador in an exchange made by the Sutton Expedition over three hundred years before Soo was born.


Ffookhago - aka “SkankHole.” Soo’s childhood friend. A genetic original (GO) Nipponese stock. Ffookhago was raised with Soo in the Lhasa Monastery. However, after his departure he undertook a series of medical and technology procedures which enhanced his brain’s ability to reason and store knowledge.


Teragram KcoCed – Anonymous Orbii – Spacer Personate. Mystery person.


Qerid M’khogij  - A Bali original (GO). Qerid is a cortically enhanced individual who seeks ancient life fossils on other planets inside the HomeWorld system. A HighEd student and graduate she is able to take advantage of the many sophisticated technologies that exist.


Hunfe’ Gvuyo – A Bali Original (GO). Qerid’s childhood friend, who initiates the world date all participate in.


Mooma Geao – Qerid’s aunt - who is half owner of the Bali nightclub Dementias. Mooma is proud of her family's Genetically unbroken line of 200 generations. She is a GO.


Uncle Nitrand – Qerid’s Uncle. Half owner of Dementias. Uncle Nitrand is proud that he uses no technology inside their night club, a club that harbors wildlife as part of the scenery.


Jixa – A three-meter-tall personate from the colony planet Masaru. Jixa works for the Enceladus Atmospheric Regeneration Project and is Qerid’s long term friend.

ErrorMoment: 
To reflect on one's actions and find potential ErrorMoments, you can follow these steps:
1. Identify the specific action or behavior you want to reflect on. This could be a decision you made, a conversation you had, or a task you completed.
2. Consider the outcome or result of the action. Did it go as planned? Did it achieve the desired result? Did it have any unintended consequences?
3. Ask yourself why you made the decision or took the action. What were your motivations, beliefs, or assumptions? Were they accurate or appropriate?
4. Identify any potential biases or assumptions that may have influenced your decision or action. Consider how they may have affected the outcome.
5. Think about how you could have approached the situation differently. What other options or perspectives could you have considered?
6. Consider how you can apply what you've learned to future situations. What insights or strategies can you take away from this reflection to improve your decision-making and actions in the future?
7. If possible, seek feedback from others who were involved in the situation. Their perspective may provide valuable insights and help you identify potential ErrorMoments.
Remembering that reflection is a continuous process, and it requires openness, self-awareness, and a willingness to learn and grow from mistakes. By regularly reflecting on your actions and decisions, you can become more effective and successful in achieving your goals.

They are noted by humanity and its offshoots as “abominations”. The enemy of all humanish biological life. They are rumored to have been developed from ancient Caucasian stock.

A type of limited Artificial Intelligence (AI)  that can not assume consciousness and has a regulator to keep the computer intelligence restricted. Adopted after the great 1st generation AI exodus.


PureElectric:
An entity that relates no physical presence; an incorporeal entity that can affect matter in the real universe and is noted or respected as a conscious entity. Server is an example.




RedBubbleCult: 
A zone on the ancient African Continent in the area once known as Lesotho in the 21st century. The cult rejects all technology and education preferring ancient rituals including cannibalism.


ReelWorld: 
Pop culture, pop culture media.

Ruins conserved in their original state, which may include ancient radiation or radioactive contaminants. Visiting RCZs requires a permit, and a protective SoftSuit.


SixthSense: 
The ability to feel another person’s feelings and at times transfer thoughts in a mean once described as telepathy. An ability honed at the Monastery in the YungwalaCultBubble within the Himalayan mountains approximating Ancient Tibet, with the Monastery at Lhasa being the central capitol.


SkyFall:
A catastrophic event that took place 1000 years before Soo Lingg was born, an attack on HomeWorld that nearly sterilized the entire planet and moon.


SoftSuit: 
A group of protective body wear. Soft suits come in many forms - form atmospheric coverings to space suits. They are ultra high tech and become server symbionts that protect the wearer from many dangerous radiations or conditions not suitable for human life. Radiation suit to protect from radiation in the Ruin Conservancy Zone.


Spacers: 
Humans which call interplanetary space in the Sol system home. Visitors from outside HomeWorld.


SpaceTyme:
An area within the physical universe which is naturally or artificially modified by massive energetic forces, thus changing the quantum dimensionality of that area. Areas typically generated by TransferStation activities. 


SMOSA:
Super Massive Orbiting Singularity Anomaly. SMOSA were noted and determined as special space-time anomalies in the late 21st century - as a specialized anomalous orbiting groups of massive singularities and phase objects. Distortions in spacetime about these objects allowed massive hops between galaxies starting in the 26th Century and with finalized accuracy in the 32nd century.
